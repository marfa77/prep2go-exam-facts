# DELE A2 (Diploma de Español como Lengua Extranjera)

- **CEFR level:** A2
- **Issuer:** Instituto Cervantes
- **Pass rule:** APTO requires at least 30/50 in Group 1 (reading + writing) AND at least 30/50 in Group 2 (listening + speaking). Max 100 points.
- **Fee:** 138 EUR (Spain (2026))
- **Accepted for:** Spanish nationality by residence: DELE A2 or higher (unless exempt) plus the CCSE test.

## Sections

| Section | Duration (min) | Weight / points |
|---|---|---|
| Reading comprehension | 60 | 25 pts |
| Listening comprehension | 40 | 25 pts |
| Written expression and interaction | 45 | 25 pts |
| Oral expression and interaction | 12 (+12 prep) | 25 pts |
