# DELF A2 tout public (Diplôme d'études en langue française)

- **CEFR level:** A2
- **Issuer:** France Éducation international (French Ministry of Education)
- **Pass rule:** At least 50/100 overall and at least 5/25 in each of the 4 papers (below 5/25 is eliminatory).
- **Fee:** varies by centre / not verified
- **Accepted for:** Proof of French A2 for a first multi-year residence card (carte de séjour pluriannuelle) since 2026-01-01.

## Sections

| Section | Duration (min) | Weight / points |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | 25 | 25 pts |
| Reading | 30 | 25 pts |
| Writing | 45 | 25 pts |
| Speaking | 6-8 (+10 prep) | 25 pts |
