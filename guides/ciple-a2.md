# CIPLE (Certificado Inicial de Português Língua Estrangeira)

- **CEFR level:** A2
- **Issuer:** CAPLE, Faculdade de Letras da Universidade de Lisboa
- **Pass rule:** 55% overall (CAPLE classification scale 55-100%). No per-section minimum.
- **Fee:** 95 EUR (CAPLE fee (all centres))
- **Accepted for:** Proof of Portuguese language for Portuguese nationality (naturalisation) under the Regulamento da Nacionalidade (DL 237-A/2006). Lei Orgânica 1/2026 (in force 2026-05-19) adds culture/history/civic knowledge requirements; the implementing regulation had not been updated by the verified date.

## Sections

| Section | Duration (min) | Weight / points |
|---|---|---|
| Reading + Written production/interaction | 75 | 45% |
| Listening | 30 | 30% |
| Speaking | 15 | 25% |

**Total duration:** about 2 hours (+15 min break)
