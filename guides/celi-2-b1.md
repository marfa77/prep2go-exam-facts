# CELI 2 (Certificato di Conoscenza della Lingua Italiana, livello B1)

- **CEFR level:** B1
- **Issuer:** CVCL, Università per Stranieri di Perugia
- **Pass rule:** At least 72/120 in the written test and at least 22/40 in the oral; pass band 94-160 (grades A/B/C).
- **Fee:** 120 EUR (Italy - Perugia centre, CELI Standard (Mar/Jun/Nov)); 65 EUR (Italy - Perugia centre, CELI Immigrati B1 / CELI 2 i cittadinanza (Feb/May/Sep))
- **Accepted for:** Italian citizenship (art. 9.1 L. 91/1992): B1 certificate from a recognised body (CELI/Perugia, CILS/Siena, CERT.IT/Roma Tre, PLIDA/Dante Alighieri).

## Sections

| Section | Duration (min) | Weight / points |
|---|---|---|
| Reading (Part A) | — | 25% / 40 pts |
| Writing (Part B) | — | 25% / 40 pts |
| Listening (Part C) | 20 | 25% / 40 pts |
| Speaking | 10-12 (+12 prep) | 25% / 40 pts |

**Total duration:** Booklet 1 (reading + writing) 2 h; listening 20 min; oral ~10-12 min

**Partial retake:** If only the written or only the oral is passed, the result can be carried for one year and only the failed part retaken (70% of the fee).
