# DELF B2 tout public (Diplôme d'études en langue française)

- **CEFR level:** B2
- **Issuer:** France Éducation international (French Ministry of Education)
- **Pass rule:** At least 50/100 overall and at least 5/25 in each of the 4 papers (below 5/25 is eliminatory).
- **Fee:** varies by centre / not verified
- **Accepted for:** Naturalisation requires B2 oral and written since 2026-01-01; DELF B2 is a CEFR B2 diploma (check the accepted-document list on service-public.gouv.fr).

## Sections

| Section | Duration (min) | Weight / points |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | 30 | 25 pts |
| Reading | 60 | 25 pts |
| Writing | 60 | 25 pts |
| Speaking | 20 (+30 prep) | 25 pts |
